How to Play: Treat Cancer Like a Boss

Note: This prototype is in active development (beta) and is not ready for broad usage as an educational application yet.

Overview

This game simulates cancer treatment decisions where you must manage therapy resistance, cell competition, and mutation dynamics. Your goal is to keep the patient alive by controlling tumor growth without letting resistant cells take over. For a quick sense of how to get started, see the "Strategy Tips" below.

Cell Types

There are 4 cell types based on therapy resistance:

  • Sensitive (Green) - Vulnerable to both effective therapies
  • Resistant-1 (Orange) - Resistant to therapy 1 only
  • Resistant-2 (Magenta) - Resistant to therapy 2 only
  • Doubly-Resistant (Red) - Resistant to both therapies

Treatment System

Six therapies are available: Paclitaxel, Carboplatin, Radiation, Tamoxifen, Herceptin, and Pembrolizumab. The tumor is randomly sensitive to 2 of these (hidden from you initially).

  • High dose - Kills all sensitive cells
  • Low dose - Kills sensitive cells with 50% probability
  • You can apply up to 2 treatments per month

Cell Lifecycle (Each Month)

  1. Treatment - Selected therapies kill sensitive cells
  2. Proliferation - All surviving cells duplicate
  3. Mutation - New cells may gain resistance (25% chance by default)
  4. Competition - Cells compete in groups, weakest die (resistant cells are weaker)

Diagnostic Tools

CT Scan: Shows tumor size in cubic centimeters (cell count / 10). Can only be ordered once every 2 months. Minimum detection: 10 cells.

Biomarker Tests: Provide clues about which therapies might work:

  • Proliferation - Indicates sensitivity to Paclitaxel/Carboplatin/Radiation
  • Estrogen receptor - Indicates sensitivity to Tamoxifen
  • HER2 - Indicates sensitivity to Herceptin
  • PD-L1 - Indicates sensitivity to Pembrolizumab

Results range from Negative to Strongly Positive based on the proportion of sensitive cells.

Win/Lose Conditions

  • Lose: Tumor exceeds 5cc (50 cells by default)
  • Win: Keep patient alive for 24 months (by default)

Strategy Tips

Mode 1: Learning Mode (Tooltips On)

  • Leave Show Cell Tooltips (Cheat) at its default of Yes. Then you can can click cells to what therapies they respond to. The red ones don't respond to anything.
  • Run a controlled comparison: in one run, use repeated high-dose therapy and observe how quickly the relevant cells that are sensitive to those therapies are killed.
  • But also notice that when mostly red doubly-resistant cells remain, they face less competition and can dominate.
  • Then repeat with more low-dose therapy to keep some sensitive cells present to maintain competitive pressure. This can lead the doubly-resistant cells to be outcompeted.

Mode 2: Clinical Mode (Tooltips Off)

  • Turn Show Cell Tooltips (Cheat) to No and play using only CT scans and biomarker tests.
  • Use biomarker tests early to identify likely effective therapies, then adapt dosing to tumor response.
  • Use CT scans regularly, and escalate to high dose only when tumor size is rising too fast.
  • In Game Settings, outcompetition pressure is controlled by Cells That Compete (group size) and Cells That Die (how many weakest cells are removed per group).

Controls

  • Arrow Keys: Pan camera
  • +/-: Zoom in/out
  • Click cell: View cell details in console

Game Settings

Modify these parameters to customize the game mechanics. Changes will take effect on the next game restart.

Starting Cells
Number of cells at game start
Proportion Resistant (%)
% of starting cells that begin resistant (orange/yellow/red, not just red doubly-resistant)
Cells That Compete
Cells are shuffled into random groups of this size; each full group applies competition (smaller groups increase outcompetition pressure)
Cells That Die
Number of weakest cells removed per group (higher values increase outcompetition pressure)
Detection Threshold
Minimum cells detectable by CT scan
Mutation Rate (%)
Probability of mutation during proliferation
Lethal Cancer Size (cc)
Tumor size that causes game over
Months to Survive
Target survival time to win
Allow Back Mutations
Can resistant cells lose resistance?
Show Cell Tooltips (Cheat)
Click cells to see their sensitivities

Note: Settings are saved to browser localStorage and persist between sessions.
